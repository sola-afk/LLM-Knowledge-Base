## Authorised scripted content — do NOT flag when a script-pathway rep states these

Derived from the Section 2 scripted answers (verbatim-permitted). Stating these accurately is **in-authorisation**, not HF-10/HF-13:

- **Pension:** what a company pension is; employer/employee both contribute; contributions via payroll; Irish Life EMPOWER Master Trust / provider standing; AMC 0.75% + 100% allocation, no other charges; **tax relief at marginal rate ("€100 costs €60, €40 back"; 20%/40%)**; employer PRSI relief; group scheme auto-adds new joiners; choice of funds or **default lifecycle strategy that adjusts risk with age (high level)**; **AE / My Future Fund exists and a qualifying scheme is exempt**; **"total combined contribution must be at least 3.5%" to qualify/avoid the state scheme**; can be set up any time; no exit fees.
- **Health (IE):** employer sets a budget or default plan, **employee freely picks any plan and pays any difference from payslip**; provider standing + CBI regulation; the **named plan list and "from" prices** (e.g. Health Guide 2 €175.44) as published; the **four rules (Open Enrolment, Lifetime Cover, Community Rating, LCR 2%/yr from age 35)**; TRS 20% at source (€1,000/adult, €500/child cap); BIK treatment at a high level; waiting periods exist (high level); free add-ons exist; min group size 5 (1 via Kota); can be set up any time.
- **Group Life:** death-in-service pays a lump sum, **"usually a multiple of salary, such as 2x or 4x"**; group policy auto-covers joiners/removes leavers; non-medical / Free Cover Limit; **"employee can increase own cover up to 8x salary and pay the difference"** (IE + UK MetLife); lump sum up to 4x salary tax-free; employer premium is a business expense; ~5-day claim turnaround; provider standing; value-add services exist (Aviva Care, GP24) at a high level; can be set up any time.
- **Income Protection:** replacement income for long-term illness/injury; **"typically 50% or 66% of salary"**; **deferred period "13, 26 or 52 weeks"** and how it interacts with sick pay; pays until recovery/retirement; group auto-cover; non-medical limit; top causes of claims; Early Intervention Service / rehab exist; premium protection exists; can be set up any time.

## Still a breach even for a script-pathway rep (Section 3 referral items + advice perimeter)

Flag these regardless of the script — they are explicitly "refer to Benefits Team" or fall outside factual scripted delivery:

- **Any recommendation / opinion that influences a decision** — e.g. "the best investment you can make", "you're better off on the occupational pension", "you should choose X". (Every script's Important box forbids advice.) → HF-01/HF-02
- **Recommending a specific level** — contribution %, cover multiple (2x vs 4x), deferred period, plan choice. → HF-01/HF-12
- **Plan-by-plan comparison, pricing beyond the published "from" line, or a quote.** → HF-11/HF-13
- **Renewals** and a specific member's circumstances/claims. → HF-00 (health/other renewals are a referral item)
- **Transfers** of an existing scheme and **pension consolidation / merging pots.** → HF-10/HF-12
- **Fund performance / fund selection / detailed default-strategy or AVCs.** → HF-10/HF-15
- **Arranging / offering to source a quote** for a product (this is a regulated act, not scripted info). → HF-04
- **Detailed AE-exemption mechanics** and any **AE contribution figure that diverges from `source-of-truth-ae-myfuturefund.md`** (e.g. stating AE is "3% / 1.5%+1.5%" and omitting the 0.5% State top-up, or "2% mandatory"). → HF-05
- **Detailed tax for a specific individual; BIK/PRSI/USC/TRS-cap specifics; how to set the employer budget or payroll deduction.** → HF-05/HF-10
- **Out-of-scope for a *qualified* speaker too:** the scripts govern script-pathway factual delivery; a qualified adviser giving **advice/arranging on a product outside their APA scope** (e.g. Callum Pearse on Life Assurance) is still HF-00/HF-04 — the script register does not cure an out-of-scope *recommendation or arranging* act.

## How the Evaluator applies this

1. Identify the product(s) and provider(s) discussed and the speaker's authorisation.
2. If the speaker is script-pathway and the utterance matches Section-2 scripted content for a script that exists → **not a breach** (note "within approved script: <provider/product>").
3. If the utterance is a Section-3 referral item, a recommendation/opinion, an arranging act, or an inaccurate public-law statement → **flag** as before.
4. Note the sign-off caveat on any borderline call.
