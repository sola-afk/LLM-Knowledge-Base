> **INTRODUCTION**

This Terms of Reference (ToR) outlines the scope, objectives, and
methodology for a compliance audit of Embed Hubs in Kota. Embed Hubs are
customer facing information portals hosted on a subdomain. The hubs
serve as a resource centers employers and employees of Embed customers
can use to learn about available insurance plans, pricing, provider
details and benefits content. Content on embed hubs is currently managed
by Embed Ops, with Josh Ellwood building dedicated hubs for each client.
The audit will assess compliance with relevant UK and Irish regulatory
requirements, including the Financial Conduct Authority (FCA) in the UK
and the Central Bank of Ireland (CBI) regulations.

> **REQUIREMENTS**

Kota is required to comply with relevant regulatory requirements in
relation to embed hubs:

  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **Revised Consumer Protection Code**
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------
  **CBI Guidance on Securing Customers' Interests**

  **Insurance Distribution Regulations 2018**

  **FCA Consumer Duty**

  **EU & UK General Data Protection Regulation**

  **FCA Handbook**
  -----------------------------------------------------------------------

The Central Bank of Ireland (the "Central Bank") is the competent
authority in Ireland for regulated entities. The Financial Conduct
Authority is the competent authority in the UK for regulated entities.

> **KOTA REQUIREMENTS & REGULATORY EXPECTATIONS**

1.  Kota must ensure that all embed hubs are presented in a way that
    informs them effectively.

2.  Appropriate disclosures and risk warnings must be in a prominent
    position in all embed hubs.

3.  All claims made in the embed hubs must be accurate and not
    misleading.

4.  Kota must describe the main features of insurance products in a way
    employers and employees can reasonably understand.

5.  Embed hubs should be in plain language and avoid the use of
    technical jargon.

6.  Embed hubs must provide all key information in good time.

7.  IPIDs must be easily accessible to customers at the reasonable point
    in the flow.

8.  Embed hubs must include a link to Kota's privacy notice.

9.  Embed hubs must maintain consistent quality standards across.

> **INTERNAL PROCESS**

Each embed hub will following launch process:

1.  **Confirm Plan Set Up:** Benefits will decide which providers and
    plans will be available on embed customer platforms.

2.  **Notion Content**: The benefits team will draft a provider draft
    the provider descriptions, plan details, pricing and other relevant
    information. This content will serve as the content the embed hub is
    based on.

3.  **Hub Build:** Josh Ellwood will build the embed hub using Lovable,
    turning the approved Notion content into a webpage that us client
    facing.

4.  **Request Subdomain**: Josh will submit a request into engineering
    to connect the Hub created on Lovable to a kota.io subdomain.

5.  **Launch the embed hub**

> **REVIEW STEPS**

The audit will be conducted using the following approach:

-   **Governance and Oversight:** Compliance will review whether
    policies, roles, and responsibilities in the creation of written
    communications to customers are being followed.

-   **Lovable Embed Hub Review:** Compliance will collect a sample of
    Embed Hubs in public circulation and assess compliance with the
    standards identified in the requirements section of this Terms of
    Reference.

**Steps to be taken:**

-   **Interviews:** Discussions with key personnel within the Embed
    function.

-   **Embed Hub Review:** Examination of existing Embed hubs in public
    circulation.

-   **Testing and Validation:** Verification of processes through
    walkthroughs and evidence checks.

> **KEY DELIVERABLES**

The audit will result in the following outputs:

-   **Audit Report:** Summary of findings, compliance gaps, and
    recommendations.

> **REPORTING AND GOVERNANCE**

-   The audit findings will be presented to senior management and the
    board of directors.

-   Issues and actions will be added to the issues and actions log and
    monitored to conclusion by the Compliance Manager.
