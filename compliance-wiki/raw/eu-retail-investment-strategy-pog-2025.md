# EU Retail Investment Strategy — Product Governance and Value for Money Changes

*Political agreement reached December 2025. Amends the IDD to strengthen product governance, introduce value-for-money assessments, and regulate pricing processes for insurance products. Implementation expected 2028–2029.*

**Source:** https://www.consilium.europa.eu/en/press/press-releases/2025/12/18/retail-investment-strategy-council-and-parliament-agree-on-package-to-empower-consumers-while-boosting-markets/

---

## Overview

On 18 December 2025, the EU Council and European Parliament agreed on the Retail Investment Strategy (RIS) — a package of targeted amendments to MiFID II, IDD, Solvency II, UCITS, AIFMD, and the PRIIPs Regulation. The package aims to empower retail investors and enhance consumer protection across financial products, including insurance.

## Key Changes Affecting Insurance Product Governance

### 1. Value for Money Requirement

**New obligation:** Manufacturers and distributors must ensure that insurance products offered to retail customers deliver **value for money**. Products where total costs and charges are not "justified and proportionate" shall **not be approved for sale**.

This introduces a quantitative cost assessment into the product governance framework, going beyond the existing qualitative target market assessment.

**How it works:**
- Supervisory benchmarks for products under the IDD will be established
- During a 4-year transitional period, national supervisors will introduce their own benchmarks
- Manufacturers must identify and quantify **all costs and charges** borne by customers
- Assessment of whether costs are **justified and proportionate** relative to the product's benefits

### 2. Strengthened Product Governance under IDD Art.25

The Omnibus Amending Directive proposes amendments to IDD Article 25 to:
- Strengthen oversight and governance rules for insurance products
- Regulate **pricing processes** at both manufacturer and distributor levels
- Require that only products delivering value for money are offered to retail customers
- Introduce the concept of **"undue costs"** — costs that cannot be justified by the product's features or benefits

### 3. Enhanced Disclosure Requirements

- More detailed and standardised product disclosures
- Improved comparability between products
- Simplified suitability assessments for non-complex products

### 4. Inducements

- New inducements tests for insurance distributors
- Enhanced transparency on distribution costs and their impact on product value

## Implementation Timeline

| Milestone | Timeline |
|-----------|----------|
| Political agreement | December 2025 |
| Final legal text | Expected Q1 2026 |
| Publication in EU Official Journal | Expected mid-2026 |
| Member state transposition deadline | 24 months after publication (~mid-2028) |
| Application date (most provisions) | 30 months after publication (~end 2028) |
| Application date (PRIIPs provisions) | 18 months after publication (~end 2027) |

## Implications for Insurance Distributors

1. **Value-for-money assessments** will become a mandatory part of the product governance framework — distributors will need to assess whether products they distribute offer fair value
2. **Cost quantification** — distributors must be able to identify all costs and charges to the customer, including distribution costs
3. **Pricing governance** — the pricing process itself becomes subject to regulatory oversight
4. **Negative screening** — products that fail the value-for-money test must not be distributed
5. **Documentation** — expect enhanced record-keeping requirements around cost assessments and value-for-money determinations

> [!warning] Upcoming
> The RIS is not yet in force. Final legal text expected Q1 2026. Firms should begin preparing for value-for-money assessments now, as implementation will require significant process and data changes.
