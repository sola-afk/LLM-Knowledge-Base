# Commission Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/2358 — Product Oversight and Governance

*This is the primary EU regulation on insurance product governance, supplementing the Insurance Distribution Directive (IDD). It directly applies to insurance undertakings and insurance distributors.*

**Source:** EUR-Lex — https://eur-lex.europa.eu/legal-content/EN/ALL/?uri=CELEX:32017R2358

---

## Scope

This Regulation applies to:
- **Insurance undertakings** and **insurance intermediaries** that manufacture insurance products offered for sale to customers ("manufacturers")
- **Insurance distributors** that advise on, or propose, insurance products that they do not manufacture

It covers the product approval process, target market identification, product testing, monitoring and review, distribution arrangements, and corrective action.

---

## Chapter II — Product Governance Requirements for Manufacturers

### Article 4 — Product Approval Process

1. Manufacturers shall maintain, operate and review a **product approval process** for:
   - Newly developed insurance products
   - Significant adaptations of existing insurance products

2. The process shall contain measures and procedures for:
   - **Designing** insurance products
   - **Monitoring** insurance products
   - **Reviewing** insurance products
   - **Distributing** insurance products
   - **Corrective action** for products that are detrimental to customers

3. The product approval process shall be set out in a **written document** (the "product oversight and governance policy") made available to relevant staff.

4. The **board of directors** (or equivalent) shall:
   - Approve and oversee the product approval process
   - Ensure it produces products compatible with the target market's interests, objectives and characteristics

5. The **compliance function** shall monitor the development and periodic review of product governance arrangements and provide advice to the board.

6. Where manufacturers collaborate to create a product (**co-manufacturing**), they shall outline their mutual responsibilities in a written agreement.

### Article 5 — Target Market

1. The product approval process shall for each insurance product identify:
   - The **target market** — the group of compatible customers
   - The **group of customers for whose needs the product is not compatible** (negative target market)

2. The target market shall be identified at a **sufficiently granular level**, taking into account:
   - Characteristics of the product
   - Risk profile
   - Complexity and nature
   - Sustainability factors (as amended by Delegated Regulation 2021/1257)

3. Manufacturers shall only design and market insurance products that are **compatible with the needs, characteristics and objectives** of the customers belonging to the target market.

4. Manufacturers shall ensure the **distribution strategy is consistent** with the identified target market.

5. Manufacturers shall take reasonable steps to ensure the insurance product is **distributed to the identified target market**.

### Article 6 — Product Testing

1. Before bringing a product to market (or significantly adapting an existing product), manufacturers shall undertake **product testing** including, where relevant, **scenario analysis**.

2. Product testing shall assess whether the product:
   - Meets the identified needs, objectives and characteristics of the target market
   - Delivers fair outcomes throughout the product's lifetime
   - Does not adversely affect customers

3. Manufacturers shall not bring a product to market if the results of the testing show that the product **does not meet the identified needs, objectives and characteristics** of the target market.

### Article 7 — Product Monitoring and Review

1. Manufacturers shall **continuously monitor** and **regularly review** insurance products, taking into account any event that could materially affect the potential risk to the identified target market.

2. Manufacturers shall assess whether the product:
   - Remains consistent with the needs of the target market
   - Is reaching the intended target market
   - Is being distributed to customers outside the target market

3. The review shall take into account:
   - Relevant information including **sales data** and **complaints data**
   - Whether the distribution channels remain appropriate
   - Whether the product remains compatible with the target market following significant market changes

4. Where manufacturers become aware that a product is **detrimental to customers**, or that the product **is not in line with the identified target market**, they shall take appropriate **corrective action**, including:
   - Informing distributors and customers
   - Amending the product approval process
   - Modifying the product
   - Ceasing further distribution
   - Contacting the existing customer base

### Article 8 — Distribution Channels

1. Manufacturers shall select distribution channels that are **appropriate for the target market**.

2. Manufacturers shall provide distributors with:
   - All appropriate information on the insurance product
   - The product approval process
   - The target market definition (positive and negative)

3. This information shall enable distributors to **understand the product** and the identified target market.

## Chapter III — Product Distribution Arrangements

### Article 10 — Product Distribution Arrangements

1. Insurance distributors shall have **product distribution arrangements** ensuring they have:
   - All necessary information from manufacturers to understand the product and its target market
   - Arrangements to distribute products to the identified target market

2. Distributors shall use the information obtained from manufacturers, together with **information from their own customers**, to identify the target market and appropriate distribution strategy at a more concrete level.

3. Distributors shall regularly review their product distribution arrangements to ensure they remain valid and up to date.

4. Where the distributor becomes aware that a product is **not in line with the interests of the target market** or becomes aware of circumstances not envisaged by the manufacturer, the distributor shall:
   - Inform the manufacturer
   - Where appropriate, take corrective action (including ceasing distribution)

---

## Chapter IV — Sustainability Amendments (Delegated Regulation 2021/1257)

As amended, manufacturers and distributors must also consider **sustainability factors** in:
- Target market identification
- Product design and testing
- Distribution strategy assessment
- Product monitoring and review

---

## Recitals — Key Principles

- **Recital 2:** Product governance requirements are "without prejudice" to any assessment of suitability or appropriateness that must be carried out at point of sale
- **Recital 5:** Product testing should include scenario analyses assessing product performance, including in adverse conditions
- **Recital 7:** Product monitoring and review should cover the whole lifecycle of a product
- **Recital 9:** Manufacturers should select distributors that have the necessary knowledge, expertise and competence to understand the product and the identified target market
- **Recital 10:** Distributors should provide feedback to manufacturers to support their product reviews
