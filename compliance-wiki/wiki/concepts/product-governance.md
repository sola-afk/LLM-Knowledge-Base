# Product Governance

## Definition
Product governance refers to the regulatory requirements governing how insurance products are designed, approved, distributed, and monitored throughout their lifecycle. It ensures products are created for a defined target market, distributed appropriately, and reviewed regularly. For [[Kota]], product governance obligations arise as both a potential product manufacturer (where products are designed or significantly adapted) and as a distributor.

## Regulatory Framework

### Insurance Distribution Regulations 2018 (S.I. No. 229/2018) — Regulation 38

Transposition of IDD Art.25 into Irish law.

**Manufacturer obligations** (applies where Kota designs or significantly adapts a product):
- **Reg.38(1)**: Maintain, operate and review a **product approval process** before any product is marketed or distributed
- **Reg.38(2)(a)**: Identify a **target market** for each product
- **Reg.38(2)(b)**: Assess all **relevant risks** to the identified target market
- **Reg.38(2)(c)**: Ensure the intended **distribution strategy is consistent** with the target market
- **Reg.38(2)(d)**: Take reasonable steps to ensure the product is **distributed to the identified target market**
- **Reg.38(3)**: Regularly **review** products, considering events that could materially affect the risk to the target market
- **Reg.38(4)**: Make available to distributors all appropriate information on the product, the product approval process, and the target market

**Distributor obligations** (applies to Kota for all products it distributes):
- **Reg.38(6)**: Obtain from manufacturers the information needed to **understand the product and its intended target market**
- **Reg.38(7)**: Have adequate **product distribution arrangements** to ensure products reach the identified target market
- **Reg.38(8)**: Regularly review whether products remain **consistent with the needs** of the identified target market

### Insurance Distribution Directive — Directive (EU) 2016/97

- **Art.25(1)**: Manufacturers must maintain a product approval process
- **Art.25(1)(a)–(d)**: Target market identification, risk assessment, distribution strategy consistency
- **Art.25(3)**: Distributors must understand products and their target markets
- **Art.25(4)**: Review obligations

### Revised Consumer Protection Code 2025 (effective 24 March 2026)

**Standards for Business Regulations:**
- **Core Standard**: Firms must **secure customers' interests** and act in their interests fairly and professionally
- Applies to both B2B and B2C relationships
- Firms must consider **customer outcomes** resulting from their actions, factoring in their role, the nature of their products, and the profile of their customers

**Supporting Standards — Governance:**
- Governance, resource, and risk management requirements for how products are designed, distributed, and monitored
- Firms must have adequate **internal processes** to ensure products and services are appropriate for the customers to whom they are distributed

**Part 3 — Information Provisions:**
- Ensure consumers are **effectively informed** (higher bar than the 2012 Code — not just sufficient information, but effective communication)
- Clear, accurate, timely, not misleading, plain language

**Part 4 — Insurance-Specific:**
- Quotation details, warranties, and endorsements presented clearly
- Premium information in same font size as other details
- Specific rules on differential pricing and automatic renewals

### FCA Consumer Duty (PRIN 2A) — UK Operations

- **PRIN 2A.3 — Products and Services Outcome**: Products must be designed to meet the needs of the target market and distributed appropriately
- **PRIN 2A.4 — Price and Value Outcome**: The price must be reasonable relative to overall benefits
- **PRIN 2A.5 — Consumer Understanding**: Communications must equip consumers to make effective, timely, informed decisions
- **PRIN 2A.6 — Consumer Support**: No unreasonable barriers to accessing support

### EIOPA Product Oversight & Governance Guidelines

- Supplementary guidance on the application of IDD Art.25
- Detailed expectations on target market assessment, product testing, distribution channel assessment, and monitoring

## Key Requirements for Kota

### As a Distributor
1. **Understand each product's target market** as defined by the manufacturer (insurer)
2. **Ensure products reach the correct target market** — not distributed to customers outside the intended market
3. **Have distribution arrangements** that demonstrate products are directed appropriately
4. **Review regularly** whether products remain appropriate for the target market
5. **Obtain product information** from manufacturers including target market definition and approval process details

### As a Manufacturer (where applicable)
1. **Product approval process** before marketing or distribution
2. **Target market identification** per product
3. **Risk assessment** for the identified target market
4. **Distribution strategy aligned** with target market
5. **Regular product reviews** considering material changes

## Our Approach
[[Kota]] distributes group insurance products (Health, Pensions, Income Protection, PEPP) across Platform and Embed verticals. Product governance obligations require:
- Target market information from each insurer partner
- Distribution arrangements ensuring products reach appropriate employers
- Regular review of product appropriateness
- Compliance with CPC 2025 "secure customers' interests" standard from 24 March 2026

## Open Questions / Gaps
- Has Kota obtained formal target market assessments from all insurer partners?
- Are distribution arrangements documented per product and per distribution channel?
- Is there a product review cycle in place?
- How does the CPC 2025 "effectively informed" standard change current practices?
