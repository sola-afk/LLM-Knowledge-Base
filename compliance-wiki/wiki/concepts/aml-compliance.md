# AML Compliance

## Definition
Anti-Money Laundering (AML) compliance encompasses the policies, procedures, and controls a regulated firm must implement to prevent, detect, and report money laundering and terrorist financing. For [[Kota]], this spans both the [[Central Bank of Ireland]] and [[FCA]] regimes.

## Regulatory Framework
- **EU Anti-Money Laundering Directives** (AMLD4, AMLD5, AMLD6) — harmonised EU framework.
- **Criminal Justice (Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Act 2010** (Ireland) — domestic transposition.
- **UK Money Laundering Regulations 2017** (as amended) — UK-specific requirements.
- **Central Bank of Ireland** AML/CFT Sectoral Risk Assessment and Guidelines.
- **FCA** Financial Crime Guide.

## Key Requirements
1. **Customer Due Diligence** — [[KYB (Know Your Business)]] for corporate clients, KYC for individuals.
2. **[[UBO Identification]]** — Identify and verify beneficial owners.
3. **[[Sanctions and PEP Screening]]** — Screen all relevant parties at onboarding and on an ongoing basis.
4. **Risk-based approach** — Apply standard, simplified, or enhanced due diligence based on assessed risk.
5. **Transaction monitoring** — Detect suspicious patterns (Kota's transaction monitoring procedures are finalised).
6. **Suspicious activity reporting** — File SARs where required.
7. **Record-keeping** — Maintain auditable records of all AML activities and decisions.
8. **Staff training** — Ensure compliance team and relevant staff are trained on AML obligations.

## Our Approach
Kota's AML programme currently includes:
- [[ComplyAdvantage]] for sanctions/PEP screening
- Product-specific due diligence tiers (see [[KYB Requirements by Product Type]])
- Formal registers (errors register, complaints register)
- Transaction monitoring procedures (finalised)
- Documented SOPs for case review and escalation

**Key gap:** No dedicated [[KYB (Know Your Business)]] provider — vendor evaluation underway.

## Open Questions / Gaps

- PEPP product risk assessment needed to finalise due diligence requirements.
- Decision pending on ComplyAdvantage: extend or replace.
