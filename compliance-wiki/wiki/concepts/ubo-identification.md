# UBO Identification

## Definition
Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) identification is the process of determining the natural persons who ultimately own or control a corporate entity. Under EU Anti-Money Laundering Directives and CBI requirements, regulated firms must identify UBOs who hold 25% or more ownership or control, directly or indirectly.

## Regulatory Framework
- **EU AMLD4/5/6** — define beneficial ownership thresholds and disclosure requirements.
- **Criminal Justice (Money Laundering and Terrorist Financing) Act 2010** (Ireland) — 25% ownership threshold.
- **EU Beneficial Ownership Registers** — member states maintain central registers (though access rules vary post-CJEU ruling).
- **[[Central Bank of Ireland]]** AML/CFT framework — requires identification and verification of UBOs.

## Key Requirements
1. Identify all natural persons with **25%+ ownership or control**.
2. Handle complex structures — holding companies, trusts, multi-layered corporate chains.
3. **Verify identity** (ID and address verification) — required for Income Protection and PEPP; high-risk only for Pensions; not required for Health.
4. **Screen UBOs** against sanctions lists and PEP databases (see [[Sanctions and PEP Screening]]).
5. **Ongoing monitoring** — ownership changes must be detected and re-assessed.
