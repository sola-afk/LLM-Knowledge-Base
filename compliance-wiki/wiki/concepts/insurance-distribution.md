# Insurance Distribution

## Definition
Insurance distribution covers the regulatory framework governing how insurance products are sold, advised on, and presented to customers. For [[Kota]] as a CBI-regulated insurance intermediary, this includes pre-contractual disclosures, product information, intermediary identification, remuneration transparency, and conduct obligations.

## Regulatory Framework

### Insurance Distribution Regulations 2018 (S.I. No. 229/2018)

Irish transposition of the Insurance Distribution Directive (IDD).

**Intermediary disclosures (Reg.23):**
- **Reg.23(1)(a)–(c)**: Identify the intermediary (Kota), its address, and that it is an insurance intermediary
- **Reg.23(1)(d)**: Disclose regulatory status — registered with the CBI
- **Reg.23(1)(e)**: Whether advice is given
- **Reg.23(1)(h)**: Nature of remuneration (commission, fee, or other)

**Pre-contractual information (Reg.26):**
- **Reg.26(1)**: Provide information "in good time before the conclusion of the contract"
- **Reg.26(2)**: Information must be in a comprehensible form to allow informed decision-making

**IPID — Insurance Product Information Document (Reg.27):**
- **Reg.27(1)**: IPID must be provided for each non-life insurance product
- **Reg.27(2)**: Must be provided in good time before conclusion
- Format governed by Commission Implementing Regulation (EU) 2017/1469, Art.1–7

**Product governance (Reg.38):**
- See [[Product Governance]] for full detail

### Insurance Distribution Directive — Directive (EU) 2016/97

- **Art.17(1)**: General principle — act honestly, fairly and professionally in the best interest of customers
- **Art.17(2)**: Information must be fair, clear and not misleading
- **Art.18**: Pre-contractual information about the intermediary
- **Art.19(1)(d)–(e)**: Remuneration disclosure
- **Art.20(4)**: Pre-contractual product information in good time
- **Art.20(5)–(8)**: IPID requirements for non-life products
- **Art.24**: Conflicts of interest management
- **Art.25**: Product oversight and governance

### Revised Consumer Protection Code 2025 (effective 24 March 2026)

**Part 3 — Information Provisions:**
- All information must be clear, fair, accurate, and not misleading
- Plain language requirement — avoid technical jargon; define technical terms where used
- Key disclosures and risk warnings must be **prominent** — not buried in footnotes or small print
- Consumers must be **effectively informed** (not merely given information)

**Part 4 — Insurance-Specific:**
- Quotation details must include warranties and endorsements in same font size
- Premium information clearly presented
- Rules on differential pricing and automatic renewals
- Consent required for auto-renewal of gadget, travel, dental, and pet insurance

**Standards for Business:**
- **Core Standard**: Secure customers' interests; act fairly and professionally
- Governance, resource, and risk management requirements
- Applies to B2B and B2C

**CBI Guidance on Securing Customers' Interests:**
- Expectations on proportionality — consider role, products, and customer profile
- Focus on customer outcomes

### FCA Requirements (UK operations)

**Consumer Duty (PRIN 2A):**
- **PRIN 2A.5**: Consumer Understanding — communications equip consumers to make effective decisions
- **PRIN 2A.4**: Price and Value — pricing reasonable relative to benefits
- **PRIN 2A.6**: Consumer Support — no unreasonable barriers

**ICOBS (Insurance Conduct of Business Sourcebook):**
- **ICOBS 2.2.2R**: Communications must be clear, fair and not misleading
- **ICOBS 4.1**: Intermediary status disclosure
- **ICOBS 6.1.5R**: Product and price information provision

## Key Requirements for Kota

### Disclosures
1. Kota identified as the insurance intermediary
2. CBI regulatory status disclosed (Ireland); FCA appointed representative status (UK)
3. Nature of remuneration disclosed
4. Whether advice is provided

### Product Information
1. Main features described so customers can reasonably understand
2. Pricing accurate and current
3. IPIDs accessible for non-life products before commitment
4. Pre-contractual information provided in good time

### Warnings
1. Risk warnings prominent and relevant to each product
2. Waiting periods / deferred periods disclosed where applicable
3. Exclusions and limitations clearly stated
4. Warranties and endorsements in same font size as other details

### Conduct
1. Act honestly, fairly, professionally in customers' best interest
2. Secure customers' interests (CPC 2025)
3. Manage conflicts of interest
4. Ensure products reach intended target market (see [[Product Governance]])

## Open Questions / Gaps
- Are all customer-facing channels consistently meeting the "effectively informed" standard under CPC 2025?
- Are IPIDs available and accessible for all non-life products across all distribution channels?
- Is remuneration disclosure consistent across all channels?
