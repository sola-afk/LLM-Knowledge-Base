# Strise

## Overview
Oslo-based "Autonomous FinCrime Department" platform. Trusted by 70% of Nordic tier-1 banks plus global institutions (PwC, Corpay, Vipps MobilePay, Nordea). Pioneered "living customer profiles" — continuously enriched entity records that unify all compliance data into one source of truth. Claims up to 90% reduction in onboarding time.

## UX & Design Highlights
- **Living customer profiles** — single source of truth combining registry data, UBO disclosures, IDV, internal systems, and analyst notes
- **User-friendly design** — reviews note "the way the data is structured makes it easy to use"
- **Pre-populated data** — onboarding flows auto-fill from registry and internal data
- **Unified dashboard** — KYB, KYC, screening, monitoring all in one interface
- **Automation + AI** — combines automation with AI-driven insights

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: Full KYB with registry data
- **UBO identification**: Uncovers complex ownership networks "in one shot"
- **Sanctions/PEP screening**: Integrated screening
- **Perpetual monitoring**: Replaces point-in-time reviews with continuous data-driven monitoring
- **Living profiles**: Continuously enriched, always current
- **Onboarding automation**: Up to 90% time reduction

## Developer Features
- **API**: Available for integration
- **Living profiles**: API-accessible unified entity records
- **No per-seat pricing**: Scales with portfolio volume, not headcount

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- Strong in Nordics and Europe
- Global institutions as clients
- Specific country coverage not publicly detailed

## Pricing
- Usage-based — scales with portfolio volume
- No per-seat fees
- Custom proposals

## Relevance to Kota
Living customer profiles are a compelling concept for [[Kota]] — a single, continuously updated record per employer combining all KYB/KYC data. The 90% onboarding time reduction claim is significant. Strong Nordic/European presence is relevant. Trusted by tier-1 banks demonstrates enterprise readiness. However, specific EU/EEA registry coverage (Ireland, Germany, Spain, etc.) needs verification. Usage-based pricing without per-seat fees is attractive.
