# Spektr

## Overview
Compliance automation platform specialising in KYB/KYC onboarding, risk monitoring, and case management. Known for exceptionally intuitive UX despite handling complex compliance tasks. Heavy use of AI agents across the workflow — KYB AI, document review AI, network discovery AI, and more.

## UX & Design Highlights
- **"Incredibly intuitive"** — praised for simplicity despite complex underlying tasks
- **KYB AI tree view** — suggests missing links and UBOs; approve or reject inline (described as "10x UX upgrade")
- **No-code workflow builder** — hyper-configurable workflows for KYC and KYB
- **Consistent UX** — same interface for individuals and businesses, integrated with monitoring and case management
- **Single platform** — onboarding, monitoring, case management, and AI in one place

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: Automated company lookups worldwide via connected registries
- **UBO identification**: Automatic discovery of UBOs, layered entities, and cross-border ownership
- **AI agents**: KYB AI, document review AI, website checker AI, network discovery AI, address checker AI, source of funds AI, industry AI, licence AI
- **Case management**: Integrated with onboarding and monitoring
- **Ongoing monitoring**: Risk monitoring and alerts

## Developer Features
- **No-code workflows**: Hyper-configurable without engineering
- **31 data registries**: Currently integrated (expanding)
- **API**: Available for integration
- **AI-first**: Multiple specialised AI agents automate compliance tasks

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 31 data registries currently integrated (expanding)
- Country-specific e-ID registry coverage
- Global lookups via partner registries

## Pricing
- Not publicly available — contact sales

## Relevance to Kota
Best-in-class UX for compliance teams — the AI-assisted tree view for UBO approval/rejection could dramatically reduce manual review time for [[Kota]]. No-code workflow builder lets the compliance team configure product-specific flows (Health vs Pensions vs PEPP) independently. However, 31 registries is significantly fewer than competitors (Vespia: 300+, Sumsub: 200+). Evaluate whether EU/EEA markets Kota operates in are covered.
