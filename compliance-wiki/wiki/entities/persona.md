# Persona

## Overview
Identity verification platform widely regarded as having the best developer experience in the space. Offers modular verification components combined into custom workflows. Unified KYB and KYC flows with business and individual verification in one system of truth.

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: Connections to 150+ official business registries
- **UBO identification**: Orchestrates every business and UBO identity record in one system
- **Complex ownership structures**: Surfaces multi-layered ownership
- **Sanctions/PEP screening**: Built-in screening capabilities
- **Dynamic flows**: Only asks for what's needed based on context
- **Automation with human backup**: Auto-processes clear cases, escalates ambiguous ones

## Developer Features
- **API**: Clean, well-documented REST API — considered best-in-class developer experience
- **SDKs**: Modern SDKs available
- **Modular components**: Pick and combine verification building blocks
- **Dynamic workflows**: Flex by region, risk, and product line
- **Unified KYB+KYC**: Single system for business and individual verification
- **Fraud signals**: Multiple data points for risk assessment

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 150+ official business registries
- Global identity verification coverage

## Pricing
- Not publicly available — contact sales

## Relevance to Kota
Best-in-class developer experience is highly relevant for [[Kota]]'s Embed API-first requirements. Dynamic flows that adapt by region and product line could handle the different KYB tiers (Health vs Pensions vs Income Protection). Unified KYB+KYC in one system is a strong value proposition. However, 150 registries is fewer than some competitors, and pricing is opaque. Registry coverage for all EU/EEA markets needs confirmation.
