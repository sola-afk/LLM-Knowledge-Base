# Financial Conduct Authority (FCA)

## Overview
The FCA is the UK's financial conduct regulator. [[Kota]] operates in the UK as an appointed representative of Innovative Risk Labs Ltd, which is FCA-regulated.

## Key Regulations & Guidance
- Money Laundering Regulations (UK MLRs)
- UK GDPR / Data Protection Act 2018
- FCA record-keeping requirements

## Relevance to Kota
- Kota's UK operations fall under FCA oversight through its appointed representative arrangement.
- KYB vendor and case management systems must satisfy FCA record-keeping standards alongside [[Central Bank of Ireland]] requirements.
- UK-specific AML thresholds and PEP definitions may differ from EU equivalents — the KYB provider must handle these jurisdictional variations.
