# Compliancely

## Overview
360-degree business identity and tax verification platform. Evolved from TIN matching to full-stack business verification and credit risk. Single dashboard for business verification, individual tax risk, and business tax risk reports. Powers 75M+ checks annually. Verifies across 44+ countries in under 3 seconds.

## UX & Design Highlights
- **Single dashboard** — run KYB checks, view tax risk reports, and manage individual verification in one place
- **Decision-ready views** — structured outputs and scoring for quick compliance decisions
- **360° business risk profile** — combines IRS signals, public records, and registry data
- **Under 3-second verification** — real-time results across 44+ countries
- **Unified audit logs** — every KYB event, decision, and override tracked

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: Real-time checks across TIN, business status, address, and watchlists
- **UBO identification**: Beneficial ownership mapping
- **Sanctions/PEP screening**: Integrated watchlist and continuous monitoring
- **TIN/EIN validation**: Core strength — evolved from tax ID verification
- **Credit risk**: Business tax risk reports with IRS signals (liens/levies)
- **Plug & play widgets**: Embeddable verification components

## Developer Features
- **API**: High-volume REST APIs
- **Plug & play widgets**: Pre-built UI components for embedding
- **Unified audit logs**: API-accessible compliance records
- **75M+ checks/year**: Proven at scale

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 44+ countries for real-time verification
- Strong US focus (TIN/EIN validation, IRS data)
- Expanding international coverage

## Pricing
- Custom based on volume and use case
- Contact sales for quotes

## Relevance to Kota
The single-dashboard approach and 3-second verification speed are strong UX wins. Plug & play widgets could work well for [[Kota]]'s Platform onboarding. However, 44 countries is significantly fewer than competitors — need to confirm EU/EEA coverage for all markets Kota operates in. The US tax focus (TIN/EIN/IRS) is less relevant for a European firm. Stronger as a complement than a primary KYB provider for Kota's needs.
