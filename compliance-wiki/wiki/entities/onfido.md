# Onfido (Entrust)

## Overview
AI-powered identity verification suite acquired by Entrust in April 2024. Covers document verification, biometric verification, and AML screening. Onfido Studio provides a no-code drag-and-drop workflow builder. Recognises 2,500+ ID types across 195 countries.

## KYB Capabilities
- **Document verification**: Automated analysis of passports, ID cards, driving licences
- **Biometric verification**: Selfie-based face matching with liveness detection
- **Sanctions/PEP/Adverse media**: Watchlist and AML screening with optional ongoing monitoring
- **Fraud signals**: IP reputation, geolocation, device integrity (passive signals)
- **Risk scoring**: AI-driven risk assessment

## Developer Features
- **API**: REST API for integration into apps and websites
- **Onfido Studio**: No-code drag-and-drop workflow builder
- **Passive signals**: Automated fraud intelligence without user friction
- **Automation**: Automated identity verification pipeline

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 195 countries
- 2,500+ ID types recognised

## Pricing
- Custom/quote-based — no public tiers
- Per-verification pricing, volume discounts
- Generally higher-priced for document IDV with manual review

> [!note] Acquisition
> Onfido was acquired by **Entrust** in April 2024 but continues operating under the Onfido brand.

## Relevance to Kota
Strong identity verification (IDV) capability but **KYB is not Onfido's primary focus** — it's stronger on individual identity verification than corporate business verification. May work as a complement to a KYB-specific provider for the KYC/individual screening side. Onfido Studio's no-code builder is appealing. Pricing is on the higher end. The Entrust acquisition adds enterprise credibility but may affect roadmap direction.
