# AiPrise

## Overview
AI-driven unified verification platform combining KYB, KYC, fraud detection, and compliance automation. Focus on deep ownership mapping, explainable AI outputs, and continuous monitoring. Claims up to 80% reduction in onboarding time.

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: Global registry search across 200+ countries
- **UBO identification**: AI-powered ownership mapping to uncover hidden relationships
- **Sanctions/PEP screening**: Real-time AI-driven sanctions checks with ongoing monitoring
- **EIN/TIN validation**: For US entities
- **Risk scoring**: Explainable AI risk decisions — audit-ready
- **Document authenticity**: Corporate document verification
- **Web & social signals**: Additional business context from online presence
- **Ongoing monitoring**: Continuous detection of risk status changes

## Developer Features
- **API**: REST API for integration into existing onboarding infrastructure
- **SDKs**: Available for direct integration
- **AI agents**: Significantly reduce manual case review time
- **Explainable outputs**: Audit-ready decision explanations
- **UBO KYC in <30 seconds**: Rapid individual checks within KYB flow

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 200+ countries for business validation
- Global registry coverage

## Pricing
- Not publicly available — contact sales

## Relevance to Kota
The AI-driven approach could significantly reduce [[Kota]]'s false positive burden — a major pain point with [[ComplyAdvantage]]. Explainable AI outputs satisfy [[Central Bank of Ireland]] and [[FCA]] audit requirements. 80% onboarding time reduction suits high-volume Embed flows. However, pricing opacity is a concern, and the platform is newer — maturity and track record should be evaluated.
