# Ondato

## Overview
EU-focused KYC/KYB/AML platform. Strong emphasis on European regulatory compliance including EU 5AMLD. Offers business registry checks, UBO discovery, and AML screening with global coverage across 195 countries.

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: Automatic registry data pull during onboarding
- **UBO identification**: Deep UBO checks and mapping
- **Sanctions/PEP/Adverse media**: Full AML screening suite
- **Credit and financial data**: Business reports, financial statements, credit scores from 195 countries
- **Due diligence**: Comprehensive risk assessment including PEP, sanctions, and adverse media
- **Ongoing monitoring**: Continuous screening

## Developer Features
- **API**: RESTful API (documented via Swagger UI)
- **SDK**: SDK integration options available
- **Document coverage**: 10,000+ document types from 192 countries

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 195 countries for global AML insights
- 192 countries for document verification
- Strong EU focus — expanded coverage for specific EU jurisdictions (e.g., Poland)
- 5AMLD-compliant

## Pricing
- Not publicly available — contact sales

## Relevance to Kota
Strong EU focus is relevant for [[Kota]]'s Irish and European operations. 5AMLD compliance is a plus. Financial statements and credit scores could support the "Check Financial Status of Employer" requirement (currently "nice to have" in [[KYB Requirements by Product Type]]). Pricing opacity is a downside.
