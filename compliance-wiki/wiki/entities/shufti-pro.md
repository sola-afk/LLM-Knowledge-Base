# Shufti Pro

## Overview
Cloud-based identity verification and compliance platform covering KYB, KYC, and AML. Access to data for 300M+ companies from 300+ data sources across 250+ countries. Known for speed (under 30 seconds) and a 90% pass rate. Offers a 7-day free trial.

## UX & Design Highlights
- **Under 30-second verification** with 90% pass rate
- **Simple single-search interface** — one search returns registration, ownership, filings, and industry codes
- **Conditional questionnaires** — tailored, risk-based forms that adapt to each business profile
- **User-friendly interface** — praised for speed and ease of integration
- **Enhanced Verification option** — adds UBOs and financial performance with expanded coverage

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: 240+ jurisdictions, company registries, business filings, ownership records
- **UBO identification**: Via Enhanced Verification tier
- **Sanctions/PEP/Adverse media**: Layered screening on top of registry checks
- **Risk-based questionnaires**: Conditional logic adapts to entity profile
- **Data depth**: 300M+ companies, 300+ data sources, 250+ countries

## Developer Features
- **API**: REST API for integration
- **No-code**: Configurable flows
- **7-day free trial**: Full access without prepayment
- **Quick setup**: Praised for ease of integration

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 250+ countries and states
- 240+ jurisdictions for registry checks
- 300+ data sources
- 300M+ company records

## Pricing
- **Setup fee**: $2,500 one-time (Starter and Standard packs)
- **Per-verification**: Custom based on volume and services selected
- **7-day free trial**: Full feature access
- Transparent public pricing available

## Relevance to Kota
Broad coverage (250+ countries, 300M+ companies) and simple single-search UX are appealing. Conditional questionnaires could handle [[Kota]]'s product-specific KYB tiers elegantly. 90% pass rate suggests good data matching — could reduce false positives vs [[ComplyAdvantage]]. However, the $2,500 setup fee is unusual, and UBO identification requires the Enhanced tier. 7-day free trial allows hands-on evaluation.
