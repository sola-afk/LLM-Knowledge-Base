# Vespia

## Overview
Estonian AI-powered KYB platform focused on speed and clean UX. Delivers commercial register data in under 30 seconds from 300+ jurisdictions — including markets most KYB providers skip. Acquired by [[Veriff]] in February 2026; integration expected by mid-2026.

## UX & Design Highlights
- **Sub-30-second verification** — median turnaround significantly faster than the industry average of 6–8 minutes
- **Web SDK** — product teams can reorder steps, add custom AML questions, or hide Vespia branding with two config calls
- **One-click PDF export** — entire case log (registry extracts, sanctions hits, analyst notes) exported for regulators
- **Clean tree view** — UBO structures displayed visually with approve/reject per node
- **In-house forms** — directors/representatives fill in info directly; Vespia connects to registries automatically

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: 300+ jurisdictions, real-time registry data
- **UBO identification**: Shareholder information from business registries
- **Sanctions/PEP/Adverse media**: Daily monitoring on all representatives
- **Risk-based monitoring**: Recheck frequency based on customer risk rating
- **Ongoing monitoring**: Daily PEP, sanctions, and adverse media checks

## Developer Features
- **Web SDK**: Configurable with minimal code (two config calls)
- **White-label**: Hide Vespia branding
- **API**: Registry and screening APIs

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 300+ jurisdictions — broadest registry coverage of providers reviewed
- Covers niche markets others miss

## Pricing
- Not publicly available — contact sales
- Now part of [[Veriff]] pricing structure

> [!note] Acquisition
> Vespia was acquired by **[[Veriff]]** in February 2026. KYB technology will be commercially available across Veriff's platform by mid-2026.

## Relevance to Kota
Exceptional speed (sub-30s) and broadest registry coverage (300+ jurisdictions) are major strengths. Clean UX with tree-view UBO display and one-click regulatory PDF export would streamline [[Kota]]'s compliance workflows. The Veriff acquisition means Kota could get unified KYB+KYC+IDV from a single EU-based vendor. However, post-acquisition integration is still in progress — evaluate stability.
