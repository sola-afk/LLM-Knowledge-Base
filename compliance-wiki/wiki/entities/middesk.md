# Middesk

## Overview
US-focused KYB and business identity platform. Retrieves data directly from Secretary of State offices and the IRS. Covers 100% of registered US businesses with 92% of records updated within 10 days. AI agents investigate ownership networks and digital footprints.

## UX & Design Highlights
- **Minimal input onboarding** — enter just business name and address to start verification
- **Intuitive dashboard** — get started immediately without API setup
- **Exceptions-only workflow** — automated decisions on clear cases; team handles only exceptions
- **AI agents** — investigate ownership networks and digital footprints automatically
- **Quick API setup** — integration in days, not weeks

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: Business name, address, TIN validation against SoS and IRS
- **UBO identification**: AI-powered ownership network investigation
- **Sanctions screening**: Automatic watchlist screening
- **Business identity**: Registration status, associated people
- **Ongoing monitoring**: Available as premium feature
- **100% US coverage**: Every registered US business

## Developer Features
- **API**: Clean REST API, integration in days
- **Dashboard**: Immediate use without API setup
- **AI agents**: Automated ownership investigation
- **92% data freshness**: Records updated within 10 days

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- **US only** — no international registry access
- 100% of registered US businesses
- Secretary of State + IRS direct data

## Pricing
- Starting at **$500/month**
- Additional costs for premium features, monitoring, high-volume
- Custom enterprise pricing for large organisations

> [!warning] US Only
> Middesk has **no international coverage**. This is a significant limitation for [[Kota]]'s EU/EEA operations.

## Relevance to Kota
Excellent UX — minimal-input onboarding and exceptions-only workflow are design gold standards. However, **US-only coverage is a dealbreaker** for [[Kota]]'s primary EU/EEA markets. Only relevant if Kota expands to the US market. Included here as a UX benchmark — the design patterns (minimal input, AI-first, exceptions-only) are worth evaluating in other providers.
