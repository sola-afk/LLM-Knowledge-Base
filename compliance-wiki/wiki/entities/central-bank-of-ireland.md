# Central Bank of Ireland

## Overview
The Central Bank of Ireland (CBI) is the primary financial regulator for [[Kota]] in Ireland. It supervises insurance intermediaries and sets requirements for AML compliance, fitness and probity, outsourcing, and conduct of business.

## Key Regulations & Guidance
- Anti-Money Laundering / Counter-Terrorism Financing framework
- [[Individual Accountability Framework]]
- [[Fitness and Probity Standards]] (S.I. No. 60/2011)
- Outsourcing requirements for regulated firms
- Consumer protection and conduct of business rules

## Relevance to Kota
- Kota is directly regulated by the CBI as an insurance intermediary.
- CBI's AML framework drives Kota's KYB/KYC obligations, including UBO identification at the 25% threshold.
- Record-keeping requirements: the CBI can request audit trails of all screening decisions, case management actions, and compliance registers.
- Any KYB vendor selected must meet CBI standards for vendor due diligence and outsourcing.
