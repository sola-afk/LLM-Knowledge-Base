# iDenfy

## Overview
KYC/KYB platform handling 2,500+ document types from 190+ countries. Known for 99.8% accuracy through a combination of AI automation and expert manual review of edge cases. Offers flexible verification workflows for different risk profiles.

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: Connects to 180+ company registries in 120+ countries for real-time data
- **UBO identification**: Registry lookups and UBO discovery
- **Sanctions/PEP/Adverse media**: One-time or ongoing monitoring
- **Credit reports**: Retrievable from government registries
- **Official company reports**: Downloaded directly from registries
- **Risk scoring**: No-code rule builder for custom risk scores based on KYB inputs
- **Accuracy**: 99.8% through AI + human review

## Developer Features
- **API**: Business Verification API and AML Screening API
- **Dashboard**: Web dashboard for configuration and key generation
- **No-code rule builder**: Custom risk scoring without code
- **Flexible workflows**: Different verification flows per risk profile and entity type

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 190+ countries for document verification
- 180+ company registries in 120+ countries
- 2,500+ document types

## Pricing
- Tiered/usage-based pricing
- Entry plans for smaller volumes
- Per-verification pricing
- Higher tiers for advanced AML/KYB needs

## Relevance to Kota
99.8% accuracy claim is attractive given [[Kota]]'s false positive pain points with [[ComplyAdvantage]]. No-code rule builder could let the compliance team configure product-specific risk rules (Health vs Pensions vs PEPP) without engineering. Access to 180+ registries covers EU/EEA markets. Tiered pricing is more predictable than pure per-call models.
