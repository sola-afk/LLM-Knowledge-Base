# ComplyCube

## Overview
UK-based KYC and AML platform with a developer-first approach. Unifies identity verification (IDV), fraud prevention, KYC, KYB, and AML into a single scalable platform. Known for flexible APIs and transparent pricing.

## KYB Capabilities
- **Company verification**: Global corporate registry search across 200+ countries
- **UBO identification**: Built-in UBO discovery and verification
- **Director verification**: Including disqualified director screening
- **Sanctions/PEP/Adverse media**: Real-time AML screening with ongoing monitoring
- **Risk scoring**: Meets FATF, FINCEN, OFAC standards
- **Ongoing monitoring**: Real-time data alerts from global registries

## Developer Features
- **API**: Full REST API with clean documentation
- **SDKs**: Available (web, mobile)
- **No-code workflow builder**: For non-technical teams
- **Flexible integration**: Choose between quick deployment and deep customisation
- **Pay-per-verification**: Transparent pricing model

## Jurisdictional Coverage
- 200+ countries for identity verification
- Global sanctions and PEP lists
- Access to corporate registries worldwide

## Pricing
- Pay-per-verification with volume discounts
- No setup fees
- AML/Watchlist screening: **$0.05–$0.80 per search**
- Scales for startups and enterprises

## Relevance to Kota
Developer-first approach aligns well with [[Kota]]'s Embed product. Transparent pricing is a significant advantage over competitors with opaque enterprise quotes. UK-based — likely strong UK/EU coverage. The no-code workflow builder could help the compliance team configure flows without engineering dependency. Disqualified director screening is a useful extra.
