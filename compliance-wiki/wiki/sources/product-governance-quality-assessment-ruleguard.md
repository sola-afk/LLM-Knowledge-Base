# How Good Is Your Product Governance? (Ruleguard)

## Key Takeaways
- 60%+ of firms struggle to track evolving regulations and maintain visibility over product performance
- Common gaps: inconsistent oversight, manual processes, poor MI, no audit trail
- Good governance requires: clear product strategy, robust target market assessment, effective distribution oversight, ongoing monitoring with MI, and a complete audit trail
- Includes a 10-question self-assessment checklist for evaluating your framework

## Detailed Summary
Practical assessment of what good product governance looks like for insurance distributors. Identifies common challenges (manual processes, lack of MI, inconsistent oversight) and defines five pillars of good governance. Includes a self-assessment questionnaire covering policy documentation, approval processes, target market definitions, review cycles, MI reporting, and audit trails.

## Impact Assessment
The self-assessment checklist is immediately usable by Kota's compliance team to evaluate current product governance maturity.
