# KYB Provider UX Research — April 2026

## Key Takeaways
- UX quality in KYB platforms has matured significantly — AI-assisted UBO tree views, exceptions-only workflows, and living customer profiles are now available.
- Smooth verification UX directly impacts conversion rates — businesses report double-digit revenue increases from better compliance flows.
- [[Spektr]] and [[Vespia]] lead on compliance team UX; [[Checkin.com]] uniquely focuses on end-user conversion optimisation.
- [[Persona]] and [[Sumsub]] rank highly on both developer experience AND user experience, making them the strongest all-round candidates for [[Kota]].
- Key UX patterns to evaluate: AI tree views, exceptions-only workflows, living profiles, conversion-first design, minimal-input onboarding, conditional questionnaires.

## Providers Researched
1. [[Spektr]] — Best compliance team UX, AI tree view, no-code workflows
2. [[Vespia]] — Sub-30s verification, 300+ jurisdictions, acquired by Veriff
3. [[Persona]] — Best combined DX + UX, dynamic flows
4. [[Strise]] — Living customer profiles, 90% faster onboarding, Nordic banks
5. [[Sumsub]] — Journey Orchestrator, 15s verification
6. [[Checkin.com]] — Conversion-first, A/B testing on compliance flows
7. [[Shufti Pro]] — Conditional questionnaires, 90% pass rate, 250+ countries
8. [[Ondato]] — EU-focused, omnichannel, user-friendly
9. [[Compliancely]] — Single dashboard, 3s verification, 44+ countries
10. [[Middesk]] — Best UX design patterns but US-only
