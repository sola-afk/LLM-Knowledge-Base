# Modern Product Governance Framework (Skyjed)

## Key Takeaways
- Product governance has three lifecycle phases: **design, distribution, and monitoring/review**
- Target markets should be assessed across five categories: customer type, knowledge/experience, financial situation, risk tolerance, objectives/needs
- Review triggers (complaints spike, regulatory change, claims deterioration) should prompt immediate ad-hoc reviews outside the annual cycle
- Product retirement must be planned — withdraw or modify products that no longer meet the target market's needs

## Detailed Summary
Framework for managing insurance products through their full lifecycle. Covers manufacturer obligations (product approval, target market definition, risk assessment, distribution strategy) and distributor obligations (understanding products, distribution arrangements, ongoing review). Includes practical steps: define policies, establish target market definitions, implement monitoring and reporting, maintain audit trails.
