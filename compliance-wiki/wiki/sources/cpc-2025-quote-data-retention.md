# CPC 2025 — Customer Quote Data Retention and Deletion

## Key Takeaways
- CPC 2025 splits retention by customer status: **6 years** for records of consumers who became customers; **12 months** for consumers who did not (e.g., quote-only)
- 12-month retention reduced from 6 years under previous Code
- 12-month period is **subject to consumer consent** — must delete if consent withdrawn
- GDPR Art.5(1)(e) storage limitation still applies — DPC suggests ~21-day "cooling off" for abandoned quotes, far shorter than CPC maximum
- CPC maximum ≠ required retention — firms must retain only as long as necessary for a specified purpose

## Impact Assessment

> [!warning] Upcoming
> CPC 2025 effective **24 March 2026**. Implementation required before that date.

**Risk rating: Medium** — Requires systems and process changes before go-live. Quote data flows for both Platform and Embed need to be mapped and automated deletion implemented.
