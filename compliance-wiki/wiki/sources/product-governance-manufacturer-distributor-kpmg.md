# Product Governance — Manufacturer & Distributor Obligations (KPMG Ireland)

## Key Takeaways
- Manufacturers must have a formal **product approval process** completed before any product is marketed
- Target markets must be sufficiently **granular** — overly broad definitions provide no meaningful guidance
- Distributors must **refine** the manufacturer's target market and may need to define it more concretely
- Manufacturers must **oversee distributors** to ensure the distribution strategy is followed — including restricting or terminating arrangements where concerns arise
- Records retained for at least **5 years** after end of product lifecycle

## Detailed Summary
Detailed breakdown of manufacturer and distributor obligations under the IDD framework, adapted for insurance. Covers product approval processes, five target market categories with insurance-specific examples, negative target market, information flows between manufacturer and distributor, periodic review, distributor feedback obligations, manufacturer oversight of distributors, and record-keeping requirements.

## Impact Assessment
Provides the clearest articulation of the **feedback loop** between manufacturer (insurer) and distributor (Kota) and the **oversight obligation** manufacturers have over distributors. Kota should expect its insurer partners to exercise this oversight and should be prepared to demonstrate compliance.
