# KYB Provider Market Research — April 2026

## Key Takeaways
- The KYB market in 2026 is mature with 10+ credible API-first providers suitable for regulated financial services.
- Most providers now offer unified KYB+KYC+AML in a single platform — single-vendor replacement of [[ComplyAdvantage]] is viable.
- Pricing transparency varies wildly — only [[ComplyCube]] publishes rates openly. Most require sales engagement.
- AI-driven verification (especially [[AiPrise]] and [[Sumsub]]) is a key 2026 trend, with claims of 80% faster onboarding and significantly reduced false positives.
- 149 countries now have UBO verification requirements, making global registry coverage a baseline expectation.

## Detailed Summary
Web research was conducted across multiple industry comparison guides and provider websites to identify the top 10 developer-friendly KYB providers. Providers were evaluated against [[Kota]]'s specific requirements for both Platform and Embed verticals.

Full analysis in [[KYB Provider Comparison — Top 10 Developer-Friendly Providers]].

## Providers Researched
1. [[Sumsub]] — Unified platform, 200+ countries, Auto KYB 2.0
2. [[Signzy]] — 340+ modular APIs, white-label, million-call throughput
3. [[ComplyCube]] — Developer-first, transparent pricing, UK-based
4. [[Ondato]] — EU-focused, 5AMLD compliant, financial data
5. [[Veriff]] — 230+ countries, Estonia-based, broadest coverage
6. [[iDenfy]] — 99.8% accuracy, no-code rule builder
7. [[AiPrise]] — AI-powered, explainable decisions, 80% faster
8. [[Persona]] — Best developer experience, dynamic flows
9. [[Alloy]] — Orchestration layer, ~500 bank/fintech customers
10. [[Onfido (Entrust)]] — Strong IDV, Entrust-acquired

## Impact Assessment
**Risk rating:** **High** — KYB vendor selection is the primary gap in Kota's AML programme. This research provides the foundation for a formal vendor evaluation.
