# Product Governance — Target Market Assessment (ESMA/A&L Goodbody)

## Key Takeaways
- Five target market categories: customer type, knowledge/experience, financial situation, risk tolerance, objectives/needs
- **Negative target market** must be defined — who the product is NOT for
- Distributors must **refine** the manufacturer's target market based on their own customer base — cannot simply rely on the manufacturer's definition
- Distributors have a **proactive feedback obligation** to inform manufacturers of relevant information (complaints, distribution data)
- Reviews at minimum annually; more frequently for complex or high-risk products

## Detailed Summary
Comprehensive guide to the ESMA product governance framework adapted for insurance distribution. Covers manufacturer and distributor definitions, five target market categories with insurance-specific examples, negative target market requirements, and periodic review obligations. Key principle: product governance is a shared responsibility with a genuine feedback loop.

## Impact Assessment
Provides the detailed target market methodology missing from the wiki's [[Product Governance]] page. The five-category framework should be adopted for Kota's target market assessments.
