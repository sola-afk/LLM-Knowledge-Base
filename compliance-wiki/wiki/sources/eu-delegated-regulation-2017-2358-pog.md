# Delegated Regulation (EU) 2017/2358 — Product Oversight and Governance

## Key Takeaways
- The **primary EU regulation** on insurance product governance, supplementing IDD Art.25
- Structured around four manufacturer obligations: **product approval** (Art.4), **target market** (Art.5), **product testing** (Art.6), **monitoring and review** (Art.7)
- **Product testing** must include scenario analysis before a product is brought to market — products that fail testing must not be launched
- **Corrective action** is mandatory when products are found to be detrimental to customers — including ceasing distribution and contacting existing customers
- Distributors must have **product distribution arrangements** (Art.10) and feed information back to manufacturers
- Amended by Delegated Regulation 2021/1257 to include **sustainability factors**

## Impact Assessment
This is the foundational regulation for [[Product Governance]]. All existing wiki content on product governance should reference this regulation. Kota's product governance framework must comply with its requirements as both a potential manufacturer (where products are adapted) and as a distributor.
