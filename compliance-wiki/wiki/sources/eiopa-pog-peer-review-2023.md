# EIOPA Peer Review on Product Oversight and Governance — 2023

## Key Takeaways
- **110 recommendations** issued to NCAs across the EU — signals regulatory expectations are tightening
- Target market definitions often **too broad** — distributors must refine them
- **Product testing** inconsistently applied — firms should request evidence of testing from manufacturers
- **Distributor feedback loop** to manufacturers often weak or non-existent
- **Value for money** emerging as key supervisory focus for 2026+
- POG supervision expanding **beyond IBIPs** to all insurance products including non-life and group

## Impact Assessment
Directly relevant to Kota. The findings on weak distributor feedback loops and overly broad target markets are areas Kota should self-assess against. The 2026 expansion to non-life and group products means Kota's Health, Pensions, Income Protection, and PEPP products will all be in scope.
