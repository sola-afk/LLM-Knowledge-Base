# EU Retail Investment Strategy — Product Governance Changes (Dec 2025)

## Key Takeaways
- Political agreement reached **December 2025** — amends IDD Art.25 among other directives
- Introduces mandatory **value-for-money** assessments — products where costs are not justified and proportionate must not be approved for sale
- Introduces concept of **"undue costs"** — costs that cannot be justified by product features
- **Supervisory benchmarks** for insurance products to be established (4-year transitional with national benchmarks)
- Enhanced **pricing governance** at both manufacturer and distributor levels
- Application expected **~end 2028** (30 months after publication)

## Impact Assessment

> [!warning] Upcoming
> Not yet in force. Final text expected Q1 2026. Application ~end 2028.

**Risk rating: Medium** — No immediate action required, but Kota should begin preparing for value-for-money assessments. This will require the ability to quantify all costs and charges to the customer, including distribution costs.
