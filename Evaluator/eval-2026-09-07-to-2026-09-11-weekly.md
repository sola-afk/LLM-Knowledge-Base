### HF-05 confirmed — the AE contribution base, stated wrong

> **Simon Ward, 23:37**: *"that 1.5% on the my future fund is taken from their **net pay**, whereas
> that 2% is taken from their gross profit."*

`source-of-truth-ae-myfuturefund.md` §5, verbatim gov.ie:

> *"Contributions are calculated on your **gross earnings**, so anything included in the gross pay
> field of a payroll file will be assessable. Contributions will not, however, be levied on any
> gross pay over €80,000."*

My Future Fund contributions come from **gross earnings**, not net pay. This is a direct
contradiction of the source-of-truth, so **HF-05 confirmed** under R3 — a specific fact stated to
the customer that contradicts gov.ie.

### This is a repeat, and it has got worse

`source-of-truth-ae-myfuturefund.md` already records the same speaker on the same point:

> **Simon Ward — LearnUpon #1 / #2 (2026-05-12 / 2026-05-15)**
> Quote: *"their pension contributions need to be based on their total ... total taxable income."*
> Verdict: **HF-05 — light: incorrect terminology, customer may apply rule to the wrong base.**

In May he said *"taxable income"* — wrong terminology, right principle, graded light. In September
he says *"net pay"* — which is **further from the source, not closer**. The coaching from the May
finding has not landed, and the error has moved from terminological to substantive.

Under R3 this is unambiguously **confirmed, not light**: a specific wrong fact, not a wording
slip.

### HF-02 — a comparison built on the wrong premise

> **Simon Ward, 23:47**: *"anybody on the sort of like higher income or higher tax bracket would
> essentially be **better off** with ... the 2% deduction from their gross pay compared to the 1.5%
> on my future fund, **even including the top up from the government**."*

This is a direct "better off" judgement between the occupational scheme and the State scheme, from
an unqualified speaker — the textbook HF-02 trigger, and HF-11 as a Kota↔state-scheme comparison.

**The aggravating factor is that the conclusion rests on the HF-05 error.** The comparison only
works if AE contributions come from net pay while the occupational scheme comes from gross. They
both come from gross. So the customer has been given a steer toward the occupational scheme that
is derived from a false premise, and told it holds *"even including the top up from the
government"* — explicitly discounting the State contribution that gov.ie describes as *"the
equivalent of 25% in tax relief"*.

Precedent: **Q4-25-FR-01** (occupational scheme framed as superior to My Future Fund) was graded
**Severe Fail**. That case also involved misleading on firm capability, which is absent here, so
Grade 4 is the proportionate call — but **Compliance should consider whether the false-premise
element pushes this to 5.** Flagged rather than decided.
