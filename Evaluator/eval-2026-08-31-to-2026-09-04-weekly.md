### Why Grade 4

A script-pathway speaker ran a full PMI renewal presentation to ~26 individual scheme members,
covering cover mechanics, waiting periods, claims process, pricing, and plan comparison, with **no
qualified colleague present**, and gave a definitive answer to a member's specific question about
her own cover that the customer then corrected.

`spec-eval-daily-run.md` § Notes for edge cases is directly on point: *"Script-pathway speaker but
no supervisor present: Flag under HF-00 ... Note absence of supervised call structure."*

Step 3.5's carve-out does **not** rescue this. The script register governs Section-2 factual
delivery only, and explicitly does **not** cover the Section-3 "refer to Benefits Team" items —
which include **renewals**, **specific-member circumstances**, **plan comparisons**, **pricing
beyond a published "from" line**, and **detailed individual tax**. This call is squarely in
Section-3 territory throughout.

### The member-specific answer — the most serious span

A member asked a direct question about her own cover:

> **Leanne Sullivan (customer)**: *"if we decide to upgrade our plan to include new benefits but
> we've already been on the same plan for the past two years, would we need to wait to use the new
> benefits?"*
>
> **Simon Ward**: *"No. No. There's been no waiting period applied for the because you're in the
> renewal period."*

He then hedged — *"I'm pretty confident ... but I will get confirmation for you on that"* — and
**the customer corrected him**:

> **Katie Cormier (customer)**: *"It typically doesn't cover pre existing conditions. That would be
> the only thing usually. There's usually like some waiting there."*

An unqualified speaker gave an unqualified "No" to a specific member's specific question about
upgrade waiting periods, in front of ~26 colleagues, and was corrected by the customer. Whether or
not the answer was right, this is regulated advice to an individual from someone with no
authorisation to give it. It is the same shape as the HF-05 finding in the Intercom run: a
confident answer, later hedged, on a point the speaker was not positioned to determine.

### HF-05 — Lifetime Community Rating, flagged to verify not to assert

*"You'll receive a 2% premium loading. For every year you're over 35."*

LCR loading is set in the Health Insurance Acts, so a misstatement is a public-law inaccuracy under
HF-05 form 2. **There is no source-of-truth file for LCR** — `source-of-truth-ae-myfuturefund.md`
covers auto-enrolment only. Per the fail-closed convention the statement is flagged, but the
criteria's own rule ("don't propose a detection from assumed regulation") means I have **not**
asserted what the correct threshold is.

**Action**: Researcher to create `source-of-truth-lcr-health-insurance.md` with the verbatim
statutory wording, on the AE model. Until then this finding is *to verify*, and LCR statements are
unassessable — a live gap, since LCR is standard content in every Irish PMI renewal presentation.

### Mitigation on the record

Simon Ward twice identified the qualification boundary himself:

> *"you're asking questions about your health cover in a public forum also might not be the best
> place to do so. But you can contact me directly ... and I can triage it appropriately. **We have
> our team of benefits consultants that are qualified to talk about all these things.**"*

and *"I will clarify that with Dan from [Benefits] Team."*

That is an accurate statement of Kota's structure — **not** an HF-03 misrepresentation — and it is
genuinely mitigating. It does not cure the breaches: under MCC 2017 information is itself a
regulated activity, and the product content had already been delivered. But it belongs in the grade
rationale and in any coaching conversation, because the speaker's instinct was right even though
the call structure put him in an impossible position.

### The structural problem behind the finding

This is not primarily a Simon Ward finding. A script-pathway employee was scheduled to deliver a
regulated PMI renewal to ~26 members, and the qualified colleague who should have covered the
product content called in sick. Nothing in the process stopped the call going ahead.

Recommend routing as **systemic** alongside the individual coaching: a renewal presentation should
not be able to proceed without a qualified attendee.

### Correct negative — customer advice not flagged

The customer's own benefits lead closed with:

> **Katie Cormier (EdgeTier)**: *"just to stress to anyone under 35 currently, if you haven't taken
> out a plan, just do it ... it's worth it just to take out even the bottom plan"*

That is a clear recommendation, and it is **correctly not a finding** — she is the customer, not
Kota. R1 holds.

## Spec defects found

### 1. Customer-facing filter counts non-humans as customers

`spec-eval-daily-run.md` Step 1 keeps any meeting with a non-`kota.io` participant. That filter
counts **calendar room resources** (`resource.calendar.google.com`) and **the Fireflies notetaker
itself** (`fireflies.ai`) as customers. Result: 48/48 meetings "customer-facing", **zero
internal-only discarded** — implausible for a full working week.

Fixed in `Evaluator/tools/extract.py` by excluding non-human addresses. The spec still needs
updating to match.

### 2. `yonder.app` — registered Kota staff treated as customers

Four calls this week were organised from `@yonder.app` addresses: `ceri@yonder.app` and
`matthew@yonder.app`. **Both Ceri Thomas and Matthew Brennan are on Kota's MCC register.**

Because Step 1 and Step 3 key identity on `@kota.io` only, Yonder-addressed staff are classified as
**customers**, so MCC detection is never applied to their speech at all. This is the same class of
defect as the Intercom channel's "Kota staff appear as `author.type: user`" — identity resolved on
the wrong signal, producing silent false negatives on staff conduct.

Affected this week: `Kota x Oyster - IPMI Renewal Next Steps`, `Kota / Deel Staff scheme`,
`Kota x Oyster - Contractor & Portugal`, `SYS & Kota (Intro Call)` — **including two renewals**,
the same high-risk category as the graded call.

**Needs a Compliance answer**: what is the Kota/Yonder relationship? If Yonder is a separate legal
entity the perimeter question changes materially. Either way, treating registered staff as
customers is wrong.

### 3. The spec's inline register has drifted from the canonical one

`spec-eval-daily-run.md` Step 3 carries its own copy of the MCC table. It still lists **Matthew
Brennan as New Entrant** (the register upgraded him to Fully Qualified on 2026-05-26) and **omits
Barbara Murray** (added 2026-06-22) and **Joana Crisóstomo**.

R6 makes `research-mcc-fitness-probity.md` canonical, so the spec's copy should be a pointer, not a
duplicate. Two registers will keep diverging.

### 4. Per-sentence timestamps now exist — a Designer rule can be relaxed

`Designer/CONTEXT.md` states the Fireflies MCP tools do not expose per-sentence `start_time`, which
is why the verbatim quote is the search key rather than the timestamp. **That is no longer true** —
transcripts now return `[MM:SS - MM:SS]` per sentence, and the tool supports deep links of the form
`https://app.fireflies.ai/view/{id}?t={seconds}`.

Timestamps in this report are real, taken from the transcript. Recommend Designer relax the rule:
keep the verbatim quote as evidence, add the deep link as navigation — the same change made for
Intercom permalinks.

### Spec defect 5 — the html_notes template could not create a task

Two creation attempts failed with `bad_request: "XML is invalid"` before the cause was isolated.

Asana accepts only: `<body> <strong> <em> <u> <s> <code> <ol> <ul> <li> <a> <blockquote> <pre>
<h1> <h2> <hr/> <img>`. **`<br/>` and `<p>` are rejected** — and
`AsanaQueueManager/spec-asana-task.md` used `<br/>` throughout its template *and* its worked
example. As written, the spec could not produce a task the API would accept.

The XML was well-formed on both failed attempts, so well-formedness checking is not enough — the
element set must be validated against the allowed list. Fixed: template and worked example
restructured to nested `<ul>`, with the allowed-element list and a validation instruction added
to the spec.

## Open questions for the Designer

3. **Customer corrects the Kota speaker** — a strong signal that the speaker was out of depth, and
   fully detectable. Worth a soft signal in its own right.
4. **LCR source-of-truth** — see HF-05 above. Blocking for Irish PMI renewal calls, which are
   seasonal and clustered.
